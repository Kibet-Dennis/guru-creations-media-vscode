GURU CREATIONS MEDIA
CAMERA POSITIONING BLUEPRINT
SAMPLE DRAFT - NOT A VENUE-SPECIFIC PRODUCTION PLAN

Use this as a starting point only. Confirm the venue layout, sight lines, audience
movement, power, cable routes, lighting, and camera permissions during a site visit.
The production lead must approve final positions and safety arrangements.

CAMERA 1 - WIDE MASTER
Position: Rear-center, on a stable tripod with a clear view of the stage.
Purpose: Continuous safety shot covering the full stage and key presenters.

CAMERA 2 - FRONT THREE-QUARTER
Position: Front-left or front-right, outside audience aisles and emergency routes.
Purpose: Medium shots of the main speaker and stage activity.

CAMERA 3 - AUDIENCE / REACTION
Position: Side or rear position approved by the venue.
Purpose: Audience reactions, room atmosphere, and cutaway shots.

CAMERA 4 - MOBILE / DETAIL (IF INCLUDED)
Position: Operated only in approved areas with a clear, safe route.
Purpose: Close-up details and supplementary coverage without obstructing guests.

SAFETY AND OPERATIONS CHECKLIST
[ ] Confirm all positions with the client and venue before setup.
[ ] Keep exits, aisles, accessible routes, and fire equipment unobstructed.
[ ] Secure cables with approved covers and avoid trip hazards.
[ ] Check tripod stability, camera sight lines, and safe operator access.
[ ] Confirm power, audio, communications, and backup recording before doors open.
[ ] Obtain required permissions before filming guests or restricted areas.

This sample is not a client-approved plan, engineering drawing, permit, or guarantee
of coverage. Replace it with a venue-specific plan after a technical site assessment.
